# 1. Introduction & Goals

## 1.1 Requirements overview

Erlangen Cricket Club runs two league teams, **ECC I** and **ECC II**. Today the club creates a Facebook event for every match and practice; members mark themselves as going or not, and captains and the board count heads by hand. Reasons for absence, practice attendance and squad decisions are not recorded anywhere.

The ECC App replaces this with one app on every member's phone. Fixtures come from the club website; practices are created automatically; members answer from a notification; captains record attendance and pick the squad; the board approves it.

### Use cases

| ID    | Use case                      | Actor              | Summary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----- | ----------------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| UC-1  | See my week                   | Player             | The coming week's match and practice of my team first ("recommended"), the other team's events below.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| UC-2  | Answer availability           | Every member       | Mark a match or practice as **Going** or **Not going**. Not going requires a reason. The answer can be changed until the deadline. | UC-3  | Help the other team           | Player             | Mark myself as going for the other team's match, e.g. when both captains agree and ask me to. | UC-4  | Reminders                     | System             | On Monday at 11:00 every member is asked about the week's practice and match, and reminded **every day at 11:00 until they have answered** or answers close (R-10).                                                                                                                                                    |
| UC-5  | Create practices              | System             | Every week with a match, one practice per team: a team with a **Saturday** match practises on **Wednesday**, a team with a **Sunday** match on **Thursday**. No match, no automatic practice.                                                                                                                          |
| UC-6  | Record practice attendance    | Captain (or admin) | On practice day at 21:00 the captain is notified with the list of players who said going, and marks each as **attended** or **absent**, themselves included. Players who did not answer or said no but came are added by hand. Anyone not marked present counts as absent. If the captain cannot, an admin records it. |
| UC-7  | Track my attendance           | Player             | See how many of my last 4 practices I attended, and what I still need: "You missed 1 practice; attend 2 of the next 3".                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| UC-8  | Attendance warnings           | System             | Each week, a player who has missed practices is notified of what they still need to stay eligible.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

After the week's practice they pick the squad from the players who said going, with eligible and ineligible players shown separately and the reason. | UC-11 | Approve the squad             | Admin              | Approve the captain's selection or send it back with a note, in time for the Thursday announcement.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

| UC-13 | Match day pack                | Captain            | Once the Board approves the squad, the captain gets the squad with every selected player's Spielerpass and the pre-filled DCB match report sheet (UC-21), to show the umpire before the match.                                                                                                                         |
| UC-14 | Make exceptions               | Admin              | Make an ineligible player selectable for one match (e.g. the team cannot field 11).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

| UC-16 | Add a practice                | Admin              | Create a practice by hand, e.g. in a week without a match when players want to train.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

The same for a forgotten password.                                                                                                                                                    |

The captain or admin accepts or declines; every change is logged.                                                                                                                            |

### Club rules

| ID   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R-1  | **Practice day.** A team with a Saturday match practises on the Wednesday before; with a Sunday match, on the Thursday before.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| R-2  | **Attendance rule.** A player must have attended at least **2 of their team's last 4 practices** to be selectable for a match: the 4 practices up to and including the practice before that match (Q-9). Cancelled practices do not count.                                                                                                                                   |
| R-3  | **Countdown.** After each practice the player is told what they still need within the current window (e.g. "attend 2 of the next 3").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| R-4  | **ECC I eligibility.** Only players the admin has flagged as ECC I eligible can be selected for ECC I (new members typically are not, e.g. those who joined in 2027).                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| R-5  | **Deadlines.** Availability for a match closes at the end of Tuesday (Europe/Berlin) unless the admin extends it to Wednesday or Thursday. Teams are announced by the end of **Thursday**, after the week's practice. Practice answers close **12 hours before the practice starts** (e.g. 06:30 for an 18:30 practice).                                                     |
| R-6  | **Reason required.** "Not going" always needs a reason.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| R-7  | **Own team first.** A player's own team's events are shown first. This is display order only, not selection priority (motion 2024/02): any member can answer for the other team's events, and borrowing follows R-16.                                                                                                                                                        |
| R-8  | **Exceptions.** Only an admin can make an ineligible player selectable, per match.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| R-11 | **When the attendance rule starts.** Practice starts before the first match (e.g. from 15 March for a match on 1 April). The rule applies once **4 practices of the season** count for a match, pre-season ones included; until then nobody needs a minimum.                                                                                                                 |
| R-12 | **Match squad.** A playing XI of exactly 11 and one **12th man**. The captain may add reserves, e.g. when the team has two games on the same day.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| R-13 | **Carry-over.** The 12th man and the reserves of a match play the team's **next match** automatically, without any selection process, ahead of the attendance rule (motion 2023/10). One who answers Not going for it forfeits the automatic place, is told so, and is selected normally from then on (Q-14).                                                                |
| R-14 | **No-show.** A selected player who fails to appear is left out of the team's next match (motion 2023/10 (2)).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| R-15 | **Availability targets.** Every player is available for at least 50 % of away matches (motion 2023/06); captains and vice-captains for at least 80 % of the season (motion 2023/05). The app shows the standing and warns when the target is at risk.                                                                                                                        |
| R-16 | **Borrowing.** A captain may pick a player from the other squad only when their own squad cannot field an XI, and only with the other captain's agreement (motion 2026/02 (4)).                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| R-17 | **Match report sheet.** Rows 1–11 are the playing XI, A1 the 12th man and A2 the first reserve, B1/B2 left for the captain (Q-15); names as registered on the Spielerpass, with the Pass Nr. A selected player without a pass number is flagged before the sheet is created. The signed sheet is uploaded after the match and kept private to admins and the team's captain. |
| R-9  | **Team squads.** Each player belongs to one team's squad (roster), assigned by the admin. There is no fixed size: motion 2026/02's "fifteen" is a guide, and the club currently has 44 registered members.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| R-10 | **Daily reminders.** A member who has not answered for a practice or match is reminded at **11:00** club time, starting Monday and then every day, until they answer or answers close.                                                                                                                                                                                       |

### Club motions

The members' General Meeting adopts motions that every member must follow (Satzung § 9 No. 1). The app enforces the ones about selection, training and availability, and every block or warning names its motion, so players and captains always see why (`domain/club-rules/motions.ts`).

| Motion  | Subject                                    | In the app                                                                                                  |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2023/01 | Training attendance (2 of the last 4)      | R-2, R-11: eligibility, countdown, "why you should go to practice"                                          |
| 2023/05 | Captains available for 80 % of the season  | R-15: captain's availability target                                                                         |
| 2023/06 | Players available for 50 % of away matches | R-15: away availability target and warnings                                                                 |
| 2023/09 | Minimum travelling squad of 12             | R-12: a match squad has a 12th man                                                                          |
| 2023/10 | Twelfth man plays next; no-shows sit out   | R-13, R-14: carry-over and no-show rules                                                                    |
| 2024/06 | New members and upper leagues              | R-4: the admin's ECC I flag                                                                                 |
| 2026/02 | Squads of fifteen; borrowing               | R-9, R-16: team squads, borrowing only when short and agreed                                                |
| 2026/03 | ECC league matches come first              | Reminder shown when a member chooses Not going                                                              |
| 2026/06 | Squad availability sheet for all members   | The app replaces the spreadsheet: who is going is visible to all members; reasons stay private (arc42 §8.3) |
| 2019/02 | Bilingual availability posts               | The app is in English and German                                                                            |

Not built: motion 2023/08 (Bundesliga squad changes only after the 4th and 8th match) is superseded by later motions.

### Roles

| Role        | Can                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Player**  | See events, answer availability, see their own attendance.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Captain** | Everything a player can (including answering for themselves), plus: mark practice attendance and select the squad for their team; see the approved squad's Spielerpasses. A member standing in as captain is given this role by the admin for that time; there is no separate vice-captain role.                                   |
| **Admin**   | The Board: club management, not team selection. Approves and announces squads, extends deadlines, makes exceptions, records attendance when a captain cannot, manages members, roles, teams and Spielerpasses; answers for themselves like every member. Captains select; the Board approves (motion 2024/04 keeps the two apart). |

## 1.2 Quality goals

| Priority | Quality goal           | Motivation                                                                                                                                       |
| -------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1        | **Correctness**        | The rules decide who plays. A wrong attendance count or a missed deadline is unfair to a player and destroys trust in the app.                   |
| 2        | **Usability**          | Answering must take one tap from a notification; captains mark attendance standing at the ground. If it is slower than Facebook, nobody uses it. |
| 3        | **Reliability**        | Notifications arrive on time, exactly once. Scheduled jobs survive retries and daylight-saving changes.                                          |
| 4        | **Privacy & security** | Absence reasons and Spielerpass images are personal data; each role sees only what it needs (GDPR).                                              |
| 5        | **Maintainability**    | One developer, a long-lived club tool and a portfolio piece: clear module boundaries and tested rules.                                           |

Concrete scenarios are in [section 10](10-quality-requirements.md).
